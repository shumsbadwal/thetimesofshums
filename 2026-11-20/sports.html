Jaiswal and Rahul lead India's response after New Zealand post 368

At stumps on day two of the first Test in Wellington, India were 159 for one in reply to New Zealand's 368. Yashasvi Jaiswal made 85 from 89 deliveries and KL Rahul remained unbeaten on 52. Henry Nicholls finished 175 not out for the hosts, while Glenn Phillips made 118.

India still trailed by 209 runs at the close, so the match remained open. Jasprit Bumrah took four wickets for 92, while the Nicholls–Phillips partnership had earlier rescued New Zealand. The score is explicitly a day-two position, not a final result; the second Test is scheduled for Christchurch next week.
