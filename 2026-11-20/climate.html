COP31 draft drops explicit fossil-fuel transition language

The revised 19 November negotiating text removed fossil-fuel language and a proposed $120 billion adaptation-finance goal. It retained a weakened electrification ambition and a reference to carbon removal. Negotiators had not yet adopted the draft; the final outcome could differ.

October's record global warmth remains the observational benchmark

Copernicus ERA5 estimated October 2026 global surface air temperature at 15.35°C, 0.90°C above 1991–2020 and 1.75°C above the estimated 1850–1900 baseline. This is a monthly reanalysis statistic published earlier in November, not a new 20 November measurement or proof that the Paris long-term threshold has formally been breached.

Extra-polar ocean temperature sets October record

ERA5 estimates put mean sea-surface temperature across 60°S–60°N at 20.95°C for October, the fifth successive monthly record for its respective calendar month. Strong El Niño conditions contributed, but the figure is a global extra-polar average, not a local Indian Ocean measurement.
