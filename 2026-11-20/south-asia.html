Nepal's prime minister prepares India visit after catastrophic floods

Nepalese Prime Minister Balendra Shah is scheduled to meet Narendra Modi during a two-day India visit beginning Monday. The trip follows destructive August floods that killed about 1,500 people and left thousands missing, according to Reuters, and it will focus in part on reconstruction assistance.

Trade, energy infrastructure and transport links are also on the agenda. Nepal seeks additional air connectivity, while India is a leading trading partner and donor. The meeting is prospective: any new financing or infrastructure commitments should be reported only after formal announcement, with promised assistance separated from money actually disbursed.

Rohingya refugees welcome genocide judgment but face prolonged displacement

Rohingya families in Bangladesh welcomed the International Court of Justice's finding that Myanmar committed genocide in 2017. Their celebrations, reported from Cox's Bazar, coexist with deep uncertainty about whether they will ever be able to return home safely.

Bangladesh faces pressure from the cost of hosting approximately 1.2 million refugees while international assistance is strained. Myanmar's continuing conflict makes repatriation a humanitarian and security challenge even after the judgment. This South Asia account centres the refugees and Bangladesh's responsibilities, rather than repeating the World desk's legal analysis.
