White House seeks to shield officials from ABA depositions

The administration asked a federal court to block questioning of senior officials in the American Bar Association's lawsuit challenging executive orders targeting law firms. The ABA alleges retaliation for protected legal work and speech; the administration rejects that account and cites executive privilege. The court has not resolved the requested depositions.

Federal court finds USDA religious emails unconstitutional

A federal judge ruled that Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins' Christian messages to staff violated the First Amendment, according to AP's 19 November account. This is a judicial ruling on government communications, not a general prohibition on individual employees' religious expression.
