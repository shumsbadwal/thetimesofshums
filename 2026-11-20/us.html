White House seeks to shield officials from ABA depositions

The administration asked a federal court to block questioning of senior officials in the American Bar Association's lawsuit challenging executive orders targeting law firms. The ABA alleges retaliation for protected legal work and speech; the administration rejects that account and cites executive privilege. The court has not resolved the requested depositions.

Federal court finds USDA religious emails unconstitutional

A federal judge ruled that Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins' Christian messages to staff violated the First Amendment, according to AP's 19 November account. This is a judicial ruling on government communications, not a general prohibition on individual employees' religious expression.

Congress confronts December funding deadline amid Iran-war dispute

Federal government funding expires on 11 December, and lawmakers are struggling to reach a deal over appropriations and the cost of the war involving Iran. President Donald Trump has sought an additional $350 billion for the Pentagon; senators are debating a defence bill that includes only part of that request.

A shutdown is a risk, not a fact. Democrats disagree over whether a funding confrontation can meaningfully restrain military operations, while Republicans have not settled on whether to pursue a party-line budget procedure. Congress must decide how to fund agencies during the transition to a new congressional majority after the midterm elections.

Senate rejection of Iran war-powers measure sharpens oversight dispute

Senate Republicans voted on 19 November against a measure directing the president to end the Iran war or seek congressional authorisation to continue. The vote came amid public concern about the conflict and debate over the constitutional allocation of war powers.

The measure's defeat does not itself settle the constitutional argument. The executive and Congress retain competing instruments over military action and spending, and the funding deadline gives the dispute an immediate institutional dimension. Reporting must distinguish a vote on war powers from an actual withdrawal of appropriated military funds.
