EU industrial policy puts Britain's China-EV strategy under strain

Proposed European-content requirements for subsidies and procurement could exclude UK-made vehicles and components. Britain wants preferential access, but alignment could require a tougher position on Chinese EV imports, creating a trade-off between industrial integration and affordable electric cars. Negotiations have not produced a deal.
