India's role in COP31 electrification dispute

The Antalya draft retained an aspirational goal for electricity to account for 35% of final energy use by 2035, but loosened language on national obligations and low-carbon supply. Reporting identified India among countries resisting stronger wording. The document remained a negotiating draft, not adopted Indian policy.
