Ramayana enters third week with extraordinary receipts and slowing daily sales

Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana: Part 1 has reached a reported worldwide gross of about ₹1,547 crore after its fifteenth day, according to figures cited by The Indian Express. Its Thursday India net collection was estimated at ₹19.35 crore, down from the previous day, demonstrating that a major cumulative total can coexist with a slowing daily run.

Box-office reporting requires care: India net, India gross, overseas gross and worldwide gross are different measures, and early trade estimates can be revised. The film's multilingual distribution and scale are commercially consequential, but these figures should not be confused with the producers' profit or a verified return on production expenditure.

Regional-language streaming slate spans Tamil and Telugu cinema

Release listings for Friday include the Tamil romantic thriller Anbil Avan on SonyLIV, the Telugu comedy thriller Anumana Pakshi on Sun NXT and the Telugu family comedy Crazy Kalyanam on Aha. The range illustrates how Indian screen culture cannot be reduced to Hindi theatrical releases.

These are platform release listings, not verified audience totals or reviews. Availability can differ by region and can change, so viewers should check the streaming service directly. For the industry, language-specific platforms and licensing strategies remain important alongside national theatrical blockbusters.

Independent filmmaker questions studios' response to a smaller film's success

The director of Hanuman Ansh publicly questioned the lack of engagement from major studios after the film's reported success, according to The Indian Express. The comments bring attention to the uneven distribution of visibility, financing and marketing resources in Indian cinema.

A filmmaker's account of the industry's response is a perspective, not independent proof that particular studios deliberately withheld support. The broader structural question is how independent films obtain distribution and follow-on investment when attention is concentrated on large, star-led productions.
