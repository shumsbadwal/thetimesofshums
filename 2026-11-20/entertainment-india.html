Ramayana box-office figures underline pan-India reach

Industry tracking cited in 19 November coverage placed Ramayana's domestic net box office around ₹844 crore after its fourteenth day, with especially strong reported performance in Kerala. These are trade estimates, not independently audited final distributor receipts; net, gross and worldwide totals should not be mixed.
