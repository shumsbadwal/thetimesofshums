Ocean alkalinity study finds mineral performance depends on dose

A September mesocosm study using northeastern Arabian Sea water found periclase increased alkalinity at modest dosing, while higher dosing triggered secondary precipitation and olivine dissolved slowly. It is experimental evidence about chemistry, not proof of atmospheric carbon removal at commercial scale.
