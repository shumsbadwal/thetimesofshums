Amit Shah offers talks and rehabilitation over disputed Siang hydropower plan

Union Home Minister Amit Shah described the proposed 11,200 MW Siang Upper Multipurpose Project in Arunachal Pradesh as a national project and offered dialogue and a rehabilitation package to affected communities on 21 November. Indigenous opposition and recent protests reflect unresolved land, ecological and consent concerns. The offer is not an agreement with protesting residents.

India and China hold another round of Arunachal frontier talks

The Indian Express reports that Indian and Chinese corps commanders met for nearly ten days after 8 November to discuss frontier tensions in Arunachal Pradesh. Officials reportedly described the exchanges as cordial, but implementation of any measures remains unclear. Talks are a diplomatic step, not verified de-escalation on the ground.

BJP reportedly restricts voter-deletion forms for Uttar Pradesh booth agents

Following complaints and police cases concerning bulk Form 7 submissions during voter-roll revision, BJP sources told The Indian Express that its booth agents are being provided Forms 6 and 8, but not Form 7, in the latest round. Claims about deletions and their religious composition remain politically contested and should not be presented as verified demographic findings.

Rajasthan local election setback exposes pressure on governing BJP

State Election Commission data analysed by The Indian Express indicate a particularly severe BJP defeat to Congress in Rajasthan's Zila Parishad elections, described as its worst in at least three decades. Local-election results cannot be extrapolated mechanically to a future assembly contest, but provide evidence of organisational and rural political pressures.
