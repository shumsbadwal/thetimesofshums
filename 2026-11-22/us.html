Reported FBI and USDA intrusions raise questions about federal cyber defences

Reports published on 20–21 November described intrusions affecting the FBI's National Crime Information Center and a USDA payments database. President Trump confirmed the FBI incident in an interview, but the extent of access and damage has not been independently established. The episodes heighten scrutiny of federal network security; they are not proof that every record in either system was compromised.
