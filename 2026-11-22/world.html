Ukraine peace talks remain open, but territorial demands divide the sides

President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said on 21 November that he was willing to join renewed peace discussions and even meet Vladimir Putin, while doubting Moscow would abandon its demand for the Donbas. He described President Trump's comments about European war spending as an observation rather than a threat. The next negotiating agenda and timing remain unsettled; willingness to meet does not establish an agreed settlement.

Iran diplomacy remains stalled despite mediated contacts

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araqchi said on 20 November that Tehran remains open to mediated talks but insists on assurances against renewed war and retaining its enriched uranium. President Trump rejected the terms as unacceptable. The IAEA's pre-war 440.9-kilogram estimate of uranium enriched to 60% must not be mistaken for a verified present inventory. Regional shipping and energy costs remain exposed to the conflict.
