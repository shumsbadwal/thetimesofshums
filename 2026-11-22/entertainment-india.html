Sonia Gandhi memoir draws exceptional early publishing demand

Financial Express's 22 November literary coverage reports publisher figures indicating more than 200,000 copies of Sonia Gandhi's memoir Belonging: A Journey of Love sold in its first week. These are publisher-reported sales, not independently audited retail totals. The release has also sparked debate about political memoir and first-person historical accounts.
