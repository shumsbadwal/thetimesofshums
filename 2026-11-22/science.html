Two NASA Mars missions pause science amid separate equipment problems

Perseverance entered a communications-related safe mode after its primary UHF radio stopped sending usual signals on 30 October; the rover remains stable. Mars Odyssey entered safe mode on 4 November after another reaction-wheel problem. Engineers are testing recovery options. These are operational interruptions, not evidence the missions have ended.

Animal study raises questions about deep-space radiation and cardiac scarring

A rat experiment using simulated galactic cosmic rays found more small-vessel heart scarring after long follow-up than in controls. The finding offers a biological risk signal for long-duration missions, but rat exposures and outcomes cannot be translated directly into predicted human astronaut disease rates.
