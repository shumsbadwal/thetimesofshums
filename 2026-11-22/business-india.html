SEBI proposes easing offshore-fund ownership disclosure rules

India's securities regulator proposed removing additional look-through disclosure requirements for foreign funds heavily concentrated in one Indian corporate group. SEBI says existing beneficial-ownership rules may suffice; supporters expect easier inflows, while the change raises transparency questions. It is a consultation proposal, not an enacted repeal.

RBI policy rate remains 5.50% in latest displayed official snapshot

The Reserve Bank of India's website showed a 5.50% policy repo rate and 3.00% cash reserve ratio in its 19 November snapshot. The same page quoted the rupee at 93.3461 per dollar at 1 p.m. that day. These are dated observations, not a verified live Sunday exchange rate or an indication of a fresh policy decision.

Tata Sons listing mandate returns to board agenda

Financial Express reports that the RBI listing requirement for Tata Sons is set to feature at the holding company's 26 November board meeting, while a Tata Trusts restructuring proposal to retain private status is reportedly not on the formal agenda. This is a forthcoming governance decision, not a completed listing or regulatory exemption.

Foreign investment debate sharpens around market transparency

Reuters reports that offshore investors sold nearly $50 billion of Indian equities between January 2025 and October 2026, followed by substantial November inflows. These reported cumulative flows help explain pressure for regulatory relaxation but do not establish that disclosure rules alone caused outflows. The proposed change still needs regulatory consideration.
