EU budget chair proposes €1.582 trillion framework and digital levy

European Council President António Costa proposed a €1.582 trillion 2028–2034 budget, €40 billion below an October compromise, and a 3% tax on certain large-company digital revenues. The proposal faces objections over farm spending, defence and competitiveness and requires unanimous agreement. Leaders are due to debate it on 26–27 November; no final budget is approved.

IEA says Hormuz disruption may permanently reshape energy trade

The International Energy Agency says the Iran war's shipping shock could accelerate diversification and electrification. Its scenario puts oil shipments through Hormuz at 8.5 million barrels a day by 2030 versus roughly 20 million before the war. These are model projections, not observed 2030 flows; continued conflict and policy responses will determine outcomes.
