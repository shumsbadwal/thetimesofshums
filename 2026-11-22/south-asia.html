Pakistan concludes Sri Lanka Test with eight-wicket win

Pakistan completed an eight-wicket victory over Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi on 21 November, chasing 90 after dismissing Sri Lanka for 316 in their second innings. The result belongs to the bilateral World Test Championship series, not an international tournament final.
