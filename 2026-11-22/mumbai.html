Maharashtra pollution board orders work stopped at Adani pumped-storage site

The Maharashtra Pollution Control Board directed Adani Green Energy to stop excavation and development work at Sawarghar village, Satara district, in a letter dated 20 November. The board cited missing intimation and necessary permissions for labour-camp and storage activity. This is a regulatory stop-work direction, not a final adjudication of all project permissions or the project's overall merits.

State withdraws legal challenge to national food regulator

Maharashtra withdrew its FDA's Bombay High Court challenge to the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India on 21 November, reportedly following a state-government directive. The case concerned the division of enforcement powers for centrally licensed food businesses. Withdrawal leaves the substantive federal regulatory dispute unresolved by judicial ruling.

Mumbai police introduce QR-verifiable identification cards

Mumbai Police announced QR-enabled smart identity cards intended to let residents check whether someone claiming to be an officer is genuine. The scheme responds to impersonation and extortion scams. Its effectiveness will depend on secure verification, rollout and public awareness, rather than the presence of QR codes alone.
