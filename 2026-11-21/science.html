Indian satellite-imagery firm SatSure secures space-fund investment

Bengaluru-based SatSure raised ₹600 million from a state-backed venture fund within a planned ₹2 billion Series B round. Its satellite and AI analytics serve finance, agriculture and infrastructure clients. Planned 2027 payload launches and a future satellite network are ambitions, not satellites already operating.

Alzheimer's study separates two damaging amyloid intermediates

A November 9 Nature Communications paper combined super-resolution imaging, single-molecule measurements and kinetic models to distinguish amyloid-beta aggregation intermediates by membrane-disrupting activity. The work clarifies mechanisms under study, but does not establish a clinical therapy or patient outcome.
