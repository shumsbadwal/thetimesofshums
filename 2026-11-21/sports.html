Pakistan beat Sri Lanka by eight wickets to take Test series

Pakistan completed a 1–0 series win in Rawalpindi, chasing 90 in the second Test after the opening match was drawn. Sri Lanka's first-innings 98 proved decisive despite a second-innings 316.
