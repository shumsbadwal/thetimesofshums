SEC accounting probe adds uncertainty for Nvidia

Trump proposes penalties for US data centres built overseas

The president said he would impose a substantial tax on American companies building data centres abroad, arguing domestic capacity is essential to AI competition. No enacted tax or detailed legislative instrument was announced; the proposal could affect global operators if implemented.

US and China discuss human control over military AI

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Trump and Xi agreed humans should remain involved in major military decisions using AI. The account is an official description of talks, not an independently verified treaty with enforcement provisions. Definitions of autonomy and practical verification remain unresolved.
