Maharashtra freezes non-essential works in drought-hit talukas

The state directed districts to suspend non-essential new construction and redirect funds to drinking-water projects, source strengthening and livestock fodder. With 310 of 358 talukas now classified as drought-affected, the policy raises questions about delivery, budget reallocations and whether essential projects can be completed before conditions worsen.

Mumbai and Thane record unhealthy air on November 20

The Indian Express reported an air-quality index around 163 in Mumbai and Thane on Friday. This is a daily reading and can vary by station and hour; it should not be extrapolated into a seasonal trend. Health advice depends on the local monitoring scale and exposure conditions.

Uttan–Virar link options reconsidered to reduce cost

MMRDA has proposed inland elevated alternatives to the Uttan–Virar project, with a claimed potential saving of ₹20,000 crore. The alternatives still need design, land, environmental and financial scrutiny. A proposal is not an approved alignment or an achieved saving.

Bombay High Court weighs firecracker restrictions

The Bombay High Court reserved its decision on a possible comprehensive firecracker ban after discussing noise, fires and misleading 'green cracker' identifiers. A reserved order is not a ban already in force; the eventual judgment and enforcement details will determine the outcome.
