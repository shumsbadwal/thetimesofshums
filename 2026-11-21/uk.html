Burnham announces UK–EU reset, but tuition-fee hurdle remains

Downing Street said negotiations concluded on timelines for a youth mobility programme, food and agricultural sanitary rules, and linking emissions-trading systems. The EU wants an agreement on European students' university fees before implementation. Prime Minister Andy Burnham's projected economic gain is a government estimate, not realised GDP; parliamentary action and detailed terms remain ahead.

Conservatives reconsider Brexit-centred opposition strategy

Senior Conservative advisers are urging Kemi Badenoch to concentrate on the cost of living, welfare and defence rather than reopen the referendum divide. The Guardian reports Europe featured only once in eight recent PMQs sessions involving the opposition leadership. This is a reported strategic adjustment, not a formal reversal of the party's Brexit position.

Youth mobility proposal puts numbers and safeguards under scrutiny

The UK–EU package envisages a reciprocal youth mobility pathway for up to 100,000 young Britons, according to reporting on the Brussels summit. The scheme's eligibility, implementation and reciprocal provisions remain subject to agreement and legislation. Its likely labour-market and education effects should be evaluated against final rules rather than the headline quota.
