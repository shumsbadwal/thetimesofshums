India announces
.62 billion Nepal flood-recovery package

Following talks on 23 November in New Delhi, Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced an assistance package worth 240 billion Nepali rupees, approximately

.62 billion, for reconstruction after Nepal's devastating August floods. Prime Minister Balendra Shah's visit also saw the inauguration of cross-border infrastructure projects. An announced package is a commitment, not proof that every rupee has already been disbursed; reconstruction needs have been estimated at several billion dollars.

Bangladesh warns Hasina will be arrested if she returns

In a Reuters interview published on 23 November, Bangladesh's state minister for foreign affairs said former prime minister Sheikh Hasina would be arrested if she returns from India in December. Dhaka is seeking extradition; India had not publicly answered the latest appeal. The announcement follows bombings, arson and protests involving rival political factions. Claims about responsibility for violence are disputed by the government and Hasina's Awami League.

Bangladesh's unrest also exposes garment workers' wage grievances

Reuters reporting from 19 November described garment workers blocking roads to demand higher wages while protests over Sheikh Hasina's proposed return disrupted transport and commerce. The labour actions were separate from the political mobilisation and should not be conflated with it. Bangladesh's export-dependent clothing industry makes prolonged disruption consequential for both workers and manufacturers.
