Shabana Azmi challenges a comparison between India and Afghanistan over clothing

In comments reported by The Indian Express on 23 November, actor Shabana Azmi contrasted India's constitutional secular democracy with Afghanistan's theocratic government during a dispute over women's clothing choices. The remarks are part of a public political-cultural argument, not a change in law.
