US restrictions on advanced AI chips reshape Chinese technology strategy

Reuters reported on 20 November that tighter US restrictions on Chinese access to advanced AI computing, including remote data-centre capacity, were affecting Chinese technology shares and cloud expansion plans. Tencent said inference workloads could rely more on domestic chips, while Alibaba warned the curbs could constrain future services. These are company assessments and policy effects, not proof that domestic manufacturing can immediately replace leading-edge foreign supply.
