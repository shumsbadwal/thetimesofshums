NASA-backed observations reveal an unusual stellar merger in Andromeda

NASA reported on 18 November that astronomers had identified a lower-mass star spiralling into and merging with a supergiant in the Andromeda galaxy. The team nicknamed the resulting object Bao and compared James Webb Space Telescope observations with other ground- and space-based measurements. The observations support a merger interpretation, while details of the star's later evolution remain a research question.

New Arctic laser-ranging station strengthens satellite-orbit measurements

NASA Earth Observatory reported on 23 November that its next-generation satellite laser-ranging system began operating at Ny-Ålesund, Svalbard, on 12 October. The instrument times reflected laser pulses to refine satellite positions and the terrestrial reference frame, supporting sea-level, ice-mass and geodesy measurements. It is an instrumentation milestone, not a new measurement of climate change.
