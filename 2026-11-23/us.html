Democrats clinch incoming House majority, but new Congress has not convened

Associated Press reported on 23 November that Democrats had secured at least 258 House seats in the 2026 midterms, with four races unresolved. That would be the party's largest House majority since 1992; the new Congress begins in January 2027, so election results must not be confused with present legislative control. Democrats have outlined affordability and oversight priorities, while Republican leader Jim Jordan vowed opposition.

Senate Republicans block Iran war-powers measure despite election losses

On 19 November, the Senate voted 51–48 against a resolution requiring President Donald Trump to end the Iran war or seek congressional approval. Three Republicans joined Democrats in support, while Democrat John Fetterman voted against. The House had previously approved the measure. The vote illustrates the gap between the incoming Democratic majority and the current Congress's power to constrain the administration.
