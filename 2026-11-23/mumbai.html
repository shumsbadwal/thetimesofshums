Bombay High Court eases Naresh Goyal's medical travel conditions

The Bombay High Court relaxed bail conditions for Jet Airways founder Naresh Goyal, allowing overseas medical treatment without requiring fresh trial-court permission for every trip, according to The Indian Express. This is a bail-condition decision, not a disposition of the underlying case.

Mumbai records mixed air quality and below-normal morning temperatures

The Indian Express reported a 23 November city AQI of 152, while Colaba and Malad registered 266 and 206 respectively. IMD minimum temperatures were 20.1°C at Santacruz and 22.2°C at Colaba, each modestly below local normals. A single day's readings do not establish a climate trend.
