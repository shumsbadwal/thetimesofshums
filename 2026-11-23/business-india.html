Indian equities retreat as financial shares weigh

November business survey finds factories offset weaker services growth

A November purchasing managers' survey cited by Reuters indicated a slight acceleration in private-sector activity, led by manufacturing demand and output, while services expansion moderated. PMI is a survey diffusion indicator, not an official GDP release.

HDFC Bank treasury chief outlines a possible rupee recovery

HDFC Bank's treasury head told Reuters the rupee could strengthen to 91.50 per dollar if equity inflows support the currency. This is a conditional market forecast, not an observed exchange rate or RBI target.
