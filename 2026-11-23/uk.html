Labour outlines a larger public role in water and energy utilities

On 23 November, Reuters reported that senior minister Louise Haigh would set out the Labour government's case for greater public control of utilities at the CBI conference. Prime Minister Andy Burnham has criticised weak regulation, high prices and sewage discharges. The government describes its approach as a partnership with private investors, not a completed programme of nationalisation. A planned speech is not enacted policy.

Cross-party MPs press for public ownership registers in overseas territories

A group of British MPs urged Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband to compel overseas territories to disclose company beneficial owners. Parliament legislated for transparency in 2018, but implementation has lagged. The British Virgin Islands argues its legitimate-interest access regime is proportionate; critics say restricted access undermines sanctions and anti-corruption scrutiny. The request is political pressure, not a new order.
