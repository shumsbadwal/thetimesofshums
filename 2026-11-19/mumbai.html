Bombay High Court challenges VIP-only civic clean-up

A Bombay High Court bench criticised Mumbai's rapid beautification around Ballard Estate for visiting dignitaries while garbage problems persist in other neighbourhoods. The observations arose in proceedings about civic conditions.

The judicial remarks highlight a measurable public-service question: whether regular sanitation standards reach ordinary neighbourhoods, rather than improving only for high-profile events. Oral observations should not be confused with a final binding order.
