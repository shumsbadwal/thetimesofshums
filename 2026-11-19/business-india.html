Economists forecast 7.6% Indian growth in July–September

A Reuters poll of 43 economists projected year-on-year GDP growth of 7.6% in the July–September quarter, with estimates spanning 6.6% to 8.2%. The previous quarter recorded 7.8%.

This is a survey forecast, not the official GDP release. Analysts cited consumption, credit and government capital spending while noting agricultural weakness and inflation risks.

SEBI weighs tighter safeguards for margin-funded share purchases

SEBI chair Tuhin Kanta Pandey said the regulator was examining safeguards for margin trading, including possible concentration limits tied to a stock's free float, Reuters reported. He emphasised that no final decision had been made.

The debate concerns leverage and market resilience: broker-financed purchases can increase exposure during rallies and amplify forced selling during declines. Consultation and consideration are not adopted regulation.

SEBI considers new safeguards for margin trading

SEBI chair Tuhin Kanta Pandey said the regulator was considering stronger protections for broker-financed share purchases, including possible concentration limits tied to stocks' free float. Reuters reported that no decision had been taken.

Margin financing can amplify both rallies and forced selling. The proposals are under consideration, not implemented rules, and should be assessed for their effects on liquidity and investor protection.

UK–India tariff disagreement raises uncertainty for Scotch importers

British ministers are seeking urgent talks over Indian customs duties on bulk Scotch whisky. Reuters reports an effective duty of 162.5% for affected imports despite the 75% concession under the bilateral trade agreement.

The disagreement turns on product classification and implementation rather than a newly negotiated headline tariff. It is a reminder that trade agreements require functioning customs administration to translate concessions into actual market access.
