Grand Theft Auto VI begins worldwide rollout

Rockstar's long-awaited GTA VI began rolling out on 18 November, with Indian availability reported from midnight on the 19th. A reported $1.5 billion in pre-orders was attributed to market intelligence estimates, not independently audited publisher receipts.

The launch is a significant test of gaming's position within global entertainment. Pre-orders, booked revenue, downloads and actual play are different measures and should not be conflated.
