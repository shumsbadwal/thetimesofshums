Homo erectus rainforest adaptation enters the research spotlight

Reuters' 18 November science reporting describes evidence that archaic humans inhabited rainforest environments in China. The report challenges overly narrow assumptions about the habitats exploited by early humans.

The underlying study, archaeological dating and alternative interpretations require closer inspection before specific claims about technological behaviour or chronology are presented as established.
