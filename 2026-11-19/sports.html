India begin Wellington Test with four early New Zealand wickets

The BCCI match centre showed New Zealand at 60 for four at lunch on the opening day of the first Test in Wellington, with India yet to bat at that checkpoint.

This is an in-progress score, not a match result. The first-session collapse gave India an early advantage, but a Test's outcome depends on subsequent innings and conditions.
