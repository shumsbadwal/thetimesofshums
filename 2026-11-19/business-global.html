Anthropic prices record-setting $81.3 billion IPO

Reuters reports that Anthropic priced 980 million shares at $83, valuing the AI company at about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis. The $81.3 billion public offering is distinct from a separately reported $26 billion private placement.

The flotation tests investor confidence in AI revenue growth despite immense computing commitments and operating losses. An IPO valuation is not proof of durable earnings or subsequent market performance.

Oil holds near $92 Brent as inventories offset shipping risks

Reuters reported Brent futures near $92 a barrel early on 19 November. US commercial crude inventories rose 3.4 million barrels to 417.8 million for the week ending 13 November, according to the EIA data cited in the report. Attacks near Gulf shipping lanes remained a competing supply risk.

The price snapshot is not a daily close. Inventory growth and expected surplus exert downward pressure, while physical disruption and insurance risk can raise the geopolitical premium. Claims by combatants about tanker attacks require independent corroboration.
