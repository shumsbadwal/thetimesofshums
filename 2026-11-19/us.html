Incoming House oversight leadership prepares investigations

Reuters reported that senior House Democrats were preparing inquiries involving Trump-family interests and Epstein-related records. Hakeem Jeffries was also nominated by Democrats for the next House speakership.

Preservation requests and political plans are not findings of wrongdoing, and a party nomination is not equivalent to election as speaker. The incoming Congress may substantially change scrutiny of the executive branch.

Federal Reserve minutes sharpen rate-hike expectations

Minutes from the Fed's October meeting indicated concern about persistent inflation and possible further tightening, according to Reuters' market report. Rates had been held steady at that meeting.

Minutes describe policymakers' discussion, not a binding future vote. Bond yields, dollar strength and global financing conditions are sensitive to how investors revise the expected path of rates.

Washington considers disclosure rules for corporate use of Chinese AI

Reuters reported that US officials were considering requiring companies to disclose use of Chinese artificial-intelligence models, citing people familiar with discussions. The proposal would use transparency rather than an outright ban.

The discussions do not amount to a published rule. A disclosure obligation could affect corporate procurement and US-China technology competition, but its coverage and enforcement remain unsettled.
