Bangladesh faces renewed political violence and garment-sector labour unrest

Reports dated 18 November describe bomb blasts and arson ahead of Sheikh Hasina's planned return, alongside clashes involving garment workers and police over wage demands. Individual incident and casualty claims require independent confirmation.

The overlap of political security and labour unrest matters in an export-dependent industry where wages, factory continuity and workers' rights are tightly connected. Reporting should distinguish workers' demands, employer positions and official security claims.

Nepal's prime minister prepares a shortened India visit

Regional reporting says Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah plans a November visit to India, with connectivity and trade on the agenda, and may shorten the trip. These are reported plans, not completed negotiations.

Cross-border power, transport and trade arrangements are important for Nepal's economic development. Specific agreements should be recorded only once both sides confirm them.
