Indian streaming slate includes Dhindora's second season

The Indian Express' weekly OTT guide lists Dhindora season two among new streaming offerings, alongside international titles. Release guides establish scheduling but do not measure audiences.

For Indian streaming, a title's platform availability and language footprint are distinct from its commercial performance. Regional-language catalogues deserve the same scrutiny as Hindi releases.
