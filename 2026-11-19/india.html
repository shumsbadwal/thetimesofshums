Election Commission outlines recourse for voters excluded from revised rolls

The Election Commission told the Supreme Court that voters left out during special intensive revision can submit enumeration forms, and that booth-level lists of deleted electors with probable reasons will be displayed again.

Winter Parliament timetable remains unsettled as delimitation bill looms

Reporting on 16–17 November indicated that the Union government may convene Parliament's winter sitting in early December, although dates had not been formally announced. Opposition representatives said they had not received a schedule. The government is seeking a path for legislation connecting constituency delimitation with women's reservation.

A possible session date is not a formal summons, and a constitutional amendment proposal is not enacted law. The bill's political difficulty lies in the supermajority required and competing regional concerns about representation.

Cyclone Akvan approaches the Andhra Pradesh–Odisha coast

The India Meteorological Department said severe cyclonic storm Akvan was moving north in the Bay of Bengal late on 18 November and forecast landfall near the Andhra Pradesh–Odisha coast on 20 November. Rail authorities were reviewing preparedness as coastal districts faced the prospect of strong winds and heavy rain.

Landfall position, intensity and timing remain forecasts, not completed events. Coastal residents and operators should rely on the latest IMD and local disaster-management bulletins rather than an edition's fixed-time snapshot.
