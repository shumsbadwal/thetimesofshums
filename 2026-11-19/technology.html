US lawmakers warn AI-enabled cyberattacks are outpacing defences

US senators warned the White House that AI-assisted cyber threats were advancing faster than protective measures, according to reporting carried in the 19 November news brief. The warning adds to pressure for stronger institutional security and incident response.

A political warning is not a quantified measurement of attack prevalence. Useful policy must distinguish automated phishing, vulnerability discovery and more speculative capabilities, and require evidence of effectiveness for proposed controls.
