Robert Pattinson is the next James Bond, ushering in Amazon MGM's 007 era

Amazon MGM Studios has named Robert Pattinson as James Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig. Denis Villeneuve is attached to direct the next film, from a screenplay by Steven Knight. Amazon founder Jeff Bezos separately said he had been heavily involved in a casting process that included screen tests. No opening date should be inferred from the casting announcement.

Beyond the celebrity reveal, Bond is a test of how a long-running British franchise changes under a new creative and corporate structure. Casting, tone, distribution and product placement will all shape the relaunch. Enthusiasm about the announcement is not yet a verdict on the finished film.

Raye and Rosalía lead a Grammy field shaped by changing pop and genre boundaries

The Recording Academy's nominations for the February 2027 Grammy Awards put British singer Raye in front with 11 nods, including album, song and record of the year. Rosalía received nine nominations, while Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers received seven each. These are nominations, not award results.

The list highlights a music mainstream that crosses pop, jazz, flamenco, country and other traditions. There is also debate over how to categorise music: BTS declined to submit material amid criticism of a newly created Asian pop category. Awards can amplify artists' careers, but they remain the choices of voting members rather than a universal ranking of musical quality.

Grand Theft Auto VI approaches its worldwide launch as a rare shared gaming event

Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto VI begins its time-zone-based global rollout on 19 November, 13 years after GTA V. Fans have preordered and preloaded the title, and employers have seen unusually high requests for time off around launch day. The game returns to a fictionalised Florida with protagonists Lucia and Jason.

The arrival matters beyond games sales: GTA has influenced online humour, music, satire and arguments about violence and media for decades. Launch-day enthusiasm and preorder estimates are not the same as audited final sales or critical consensus. Performance and the experience of ordinary players will become clearer as the rollout proceeds.

Governors Awards honour Glenn Close and Ridley Scott as Hollywood's awards season takes shape

Glenn Close and director Ridley Scott were among five industry figures recognised at the Academy's Governors Awards on Sunday. The event brought prominent film artists together early in the awards-season calendar; Scott accepted his honour remotely and paid tribute to his late brother, filmmaker Tony Scott.

Honorary Oscars recognise careers rather than the merit of a particular new film. The ceremony also illustrates how awards season combines remembrance, industry relationships and campaigning for upcoming competitive prizes. It should not be confused with the main Academy Awards results.

Disney plans $26 billion in content spending as streaming competes with other entertainment

Disney said it expects to spend about $26 billion in cash on content in its next fiscal year, a 9% increase, after quarterly results were helped by theme parks and NFL audiences. The plans include international programming and sports rights. The company operates Disney+, while also running film, television, sports and theme-park businesses.

The spending decision reflects an industry in which subscription platforms, live sports, cinema, short-form video and real-world attractions compete for attention. Higher spending alone does not guarantee more hits or sustainable margins. The eventual mix of programming and how audiences respond will matter more than the budget headline.

Entertainment and Pop Culture - GlobalFilm industry · StudiosStory 6 of 8

Skydance sets leadership for Paramount and Warner Bros film labels

After its Warner Bros Discovery takeover, Skydance announced a new film leadership structure: Don Granger and Kira Goldberg will lead the Paramount Pictures motion-picture group, while Jesse Ehrman becomes president of the Warner Bros Pictures motion-picture group. The company has described an ambition to produce 30 films a year across distinct studio labels.

A combined media company may gain scale in financing, marketing and distribution, yet the question for filmmakers and audiences is whether the labels maintain varied creative identities. Announced production targets are ambitions, not completed release slates. Industry concentration also deserves scrutiny alongside possible operating efficiencies.

BTS and Spider-Man fare strongly in the fan-voted Kids' Choice Awards

At Nickelodeon's 2026 Kids' Choice Awards, BTS won favourite music group, favourite album for ARIRANG and favourite song for Swim. Spider-Man: Brand New Day won favourite movie; Tom Holland and Zendaya were recognised for acting, and Chris Pratt received a comedy honour.

The fan-voted awards measure a particular kind of cultural engagement: communities mobilising around stars and franchises. They are valuable evidence of audience enthusiasm but should not be presented as box-office, streaming or critical rankings. The event's CBS and Paramount+ distribution also shows how media companies try to bring youth-oriented entertainment across platforms.

Entertainment and Pop Culture - GlobalCinema · Workplace cultureStory 8 of 8

Depardieu withdraws sexual-assault appeal, revisiting accountability on film sets

French actor Gérard Depardieu withdrew his appeal against a 2025 sexual-assault conviction involving two women on a film set and publicly apologised. The 18-month suspended sentence followed the earlier conviction. Reuters also reports that he faces a separate prospective trial over an alleged rape, which he denies; that allegation must not be treated as a conviction.

The development is part of a broader reassessment of power and workplace protections in the film industry. It raises questions about reporting channels, safety on sets and whether professional stature affects accountability. Distinguishing the settled appeal from the separate allegation is essential for accurate coverage.
